Drip Variations

Same hybrid base — different drip treatments. Enlarged + actual sizes.

A — Beading Drip

Thin neck pinches into a separate bead forming at the bottom. Like sap about to detach from a branch.

B — Sticky Thread

Long viscous strand curving off the bottom with a tiny satellite droplet clinging to it. Very sap-like — that gooey pull when you touch a pine tree.

C — Scatter Drops

Short drip plus tiny satellite droplets scattered around the base — like sap that just hit a surface. More dynamic, splashy energy.
