Ink Splatter Style

What vibe should the splatters have on the cream background?

Dark Ink

Black/sepia ink blots — like a fountain pen accident. Moody, scholarly.

Coffee Stains

Warm brown rings and drips — like a mug left on paper. Cozy, lived-in.

Pulp Amber

Orange/amber splatters matching Pulp's accent color. On-brand, like citrus juice stains.

Mixed Desk

All three together — ink, coffee, and amber. Layered, messy-desk energy.
