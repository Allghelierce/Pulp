# Pulp Rework Ideas

## Currency: Time
- Single currency: time. 1:1 ratio — minutes focused = time earned
- After growing a tree, user picks: keep the tree OR collect time
- Keep = tree goes in seasonal orchard, produces passive sap
- Collect = instant time payout
- **Time** = 1:1 earned, pure flex, leaderboard currency. Buys functional seeds.
- **Lifetime time earned** = leaderboard score. Never goes down. Pure measure of effort.
- **Time balance** = spendable wallet. Goes up and down as you buy seeds.
- **Sap** = produced by kept trees, spent in seasonal night market. Gameplay/cosmetic currency. NOT competed for.

## Orchard
- Two spaces, same terrain/mountains extended, seamless swipe transition with arrow
- **Seasonal orchard** (left) — earned trees from focus sessions, produce sap, resets quarterly (Sep-Nov, Dec-Feb, Mar-May, Jun-Aug)
- **Arrow →** swipe right to transition
- **Personal garden** (right) — trees bought from night market, permanent, **produce XP**
  - More personal garden trees = higher XP = higher bracket = tougher competition (natural balance)
- **Trophy strip** — far left of seasonal orchard. Pick 1 tree per season to keep forever. Timeline of your journey.
- **Season passport** — permanent snapshot of each season's final orchard, rank, and stats. Viewable anytime.

## Trees
- ~10 permanent core trees, always available
- 3-5 seasonal exclusives per quarter (themed: spring blossoms, winter frost, etc.)
- Rotate 1-2 old seasonals back occasionally for scarcity

## Competitions
- Strava model — many small competitions anyone can win
- 3-4 active per season, rotated
- All measure **delta not absolute** — everyone starts at zero each season
- Level-based brackets: Bronze / Silver / Gold / Diamond (by peak XP, no deranking)

### Competition Ideas (pick 3-4 per season)
**Time-based**
- Sprint week — most minutes in 7 days
- Streak survivor — longest daily streak from competition start
- Consistency king — lowest variance in daily focus time
- Marathon — longest single session
- Comeback kid — biggest week-over-week improvement

**Learning-science-backed**
- Quiz master — most flashcards reviewed
- Recall streak — consecutive days reviewing flashcards
- Returner — most revisits to old notebooks
- Cornell convert — most cornell-format notes created
- Pre-exam ramp — increasing study time as tagged exam date approaches

## Goal Commitment
- Pick a daily tier: 15min, 30min, or 90min
- Get a time multiplier based on tier
- Break it = lose streak multiplier (not full wipe)

## Shops
- **Seed shop** (time) — functional trees that produce sap. Always available.
- **Night market** (sap) — Valorant-style rotating shop
  - 5 mystery slots, each a face-down card
  - Click to flip/unveil with animation
  - Resets twice daily with countdown timer
  - Items weighted by rarity — common stuff frequent, seasonal exclusives rare
  - Once unveiled, buy or leave. No re-rolls.
  - Sells: cosmetic trees (no sap, but produce XP in personal garden), tree auras/particles, orchard terrain, profile flair, notebook themes, timer skins, sound packs, app themes
  - Trees bought here go to personal garden permanently

## Seasonal Orchard Layout
- All tills visible from day one (empty plots = visual motivation)
- Trees plant left to right in columns
- Free players: ~40 tree cap (8-10 columns x 4-5 trees)
- Pro: unlimited columns

## Groups & Leaderboards
- Leaderboards compete on **lifetime time earned** — pure, uncheateable, no advantage from spending
- **Study rooms** — user-created groups with invite codes. Friends, classmates compete privately. Can be in multiple groups at once. Every invite code is viral distribution.
- **Bracket lobbies** — within your bracket, randomly assigned ~50 person lobby each season.
- All groups are layers on top — same app, same trees, same orchard. Groups just add leaderboard tabs.
- Joining a group retroactively counts existing focus time. Leaving removes the tab, nothing else.
- Classes (from teacher plan) are just another group tab — student's app doesn't change.

## Pro Subscription
- Unlimited orchard columns (free caps at ~40 trees)
- Night market: 7 slots instead of 5
- Third daily night market reset
- Advanced stats page with analytics, time breakdowns, study heatmaps
- Trophy strip holds 3 trees per season instead of 1
- Expanded personal garden terrain
- Ad-reduced

## B2B Education Tier (Teacher Plan)
- Teacher pays for **visibility**, not student upgrades. Students stay free tier.
- **Teacher dashboard** — create a class, get a code, students join
- Teacher sees focus time, streaks, orchard progress per student
- **Class leaderboard** — built-in competition without teacher effort
- **Class goals** — "everyone hit 30min before Friday", track completion
- **Assignment tagging** — link focus time to specific assignments, see who's studying what
- **Engagement reports** — exportable for grades/participation credit
- **Bulk class creation** — multiple sections, semesters
- Students join via code in settings. Existing activity counts immediately. Teacher only sees time + streaks, NOT notebook content (privacy).

## Go-To-Market
- Target: college students and high school AP teachers (NOT elementary/middle school)
- Start at UCSD — one professor, one class, this quarter
- Study room invite codes = built-in viral loop
- Orchard screenshots are inherently shareable (TikTok/Instagram)
- Teachers are free distribution — they assign it, 30 students download, friends spread it

## Revenue Model
- **Pro sub**: $5/month or $40/year
- **Teacher plan**: $3-5/student/semester
- **Ads**: free tier, interstitial after timer + banner in shop
- Never sell time. Never sell sap. Never sell competitive advantage.
