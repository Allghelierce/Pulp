# Pulp Economy v2

## Currency: Sap (single)
- Earned on session completion — all grove trees yield + new tree yield
- No collect button, automatic on session end
- Gems killed, hibernation killed

## Seeds: Consumable
- Tangerine always free
- Every other seed costs sap, consumed on planting
- Trees can die during timer from failed watering or giving up

| Rarity | Seed cost | sapYield | Weekly upkeep |
|--------|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Common | Free | 8 | 4 |
| Uncommon | 80 | 16 | 8 |
| Rare | 200 | 32 | 16 |
| True rare | 2000 | 60 | 32 |
| Sacred | 8000 | 160 | 80 |

## Daily Goal = Difficulty Slider
- Higher goal = higher sap multiplier
- Higher goal = higher weekly upkeep
- Multiplier stacks with streak + morning bonus (up to 3x peak)

## Streaks = Life Support
- Active streak discounts upkeep (scales with streak length, caps at ~80% off)
- Break streak = sap drains to zero, trees go dormant
- Trees never die from upkeep — they sleep
- Come back, rebuild streak = trees wake up one by one
- Day 1 back: best tree wakes. Day 3: a few more. Day 14+: full grove

## Dormancy
- Dormant trees produce 0 sap
- Trees don't die, grove is preserved
- Punishment = lost sap (wallet), not lost assets (trees)
- Sap is easy to re-earn once focusing againhmm

## Competitions
- League tiers (bronze-diamond) grouped by sap delta (production rate per 5min)
- Weekly leaderboard: sap earned within tier
- Monthly leaderboard: focus minutes within tier
- Build better grove = move up tiers
