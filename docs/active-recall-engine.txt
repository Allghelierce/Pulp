PULP — ACTIVE RECALL ENGINE PLAN
================================

Where we are
------------
Recall mode already has the skeleton: AI card generation (Groq, api/recall),
SM-2 spaced-repetition scheduling, self-grading (Again/Hard/Good/Easy), and
correct answers grow the notebook's tree (slice 1, feature/recall-mode).

What makes it an *engine*: shifting from RECOGNITION (flip card, rate yourself)
to PRODUCTION + JUDGMENT (you generate the answer, the system grades it and
models what you know).


The keystone upgrade: produce-then-grade
----------------------------------------
Today you flip and self-rate — that's recognition, and it's gameable (you can
just click "Good" to farm growth). Instead: the user TYPES (or speaks) the
answer, then AI grades it semantically against the expected answer:
correct / partial / wrong + one line of feedback. This single change turns
flashcards into real active recall, and makes tree growth honest — you grow
from actual retrieval, not clicks.


The engine, in layers
---------------------
1. Concept extraction — AI parses a notebook into atomic concepts plus the
   source span each came from (so answers can cite the note). Mastery is
   tracked per CONCEPT, not per card.

2. Question generator — per concept, multiple forms at tiered difficulty:
   free recall, cloze (fill-in-the-blank), "explain in your own words," and
   application/transfer questions. Bloom's ladder: recall -> understand -> apply.

3. Grader — semantic AI grading of free-text answers, structured feedback,
   and a hint-first retry when wrong.

4. Mastery model — per-concept strength / next-due (SM-2 today, or upgrade to
   FSRS). Persisted per user, CROSS-NOTEBOOK.

5. Scheduler — picks the next item to maximize learning: due reviews + weak
   concepts + interleaving (mix topics, don't mass). Surfaces a "today's
   recall" queue across all notebooks, not just per-notebook.

6. Reward tie-in — MASTERY GAINS (a due item recalled correctly) drive tree
   growth and sap, not raw correct count.


Build order
-----------
Slice 2 — Typed answer + AI grading (the keystone; biggest leverage)
  - ReviewView: "type your answer" input; grade after submit instead of pure
    self-rate.
  - New /api/recall/grade endpoint: AI compares the user's answer to the
    expected answer -> correct / partial / wrong + one-line feedback.
  - Growth scales with the grade (full for correct, partial for partial, none
    for wrong) — replaces the clickable self-grade so it can't be farmed.

Slice 3 — Concept + mastery model, cross-notebook "due today."

Slice 4 — Question variety + adaptive difficulty (cloze, application,
          harder-when-mastered).

Slice 5 — Feedback, hints, source citations, confidence calibration.


Model choices
-------------
- Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) for latency-sensitive paths (card gen, grading).
- Gemini Flash / Claude Haiku 4.5 as options where quality / structured output
  matters more than speed.
- Estimated AI cost: ~$0.12 per active paying user per month — pricing does
  not need to change; add per-user AI caps and gate heavy AI to paid.
